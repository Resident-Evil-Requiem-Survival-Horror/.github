# Resident Evil Requiem: Survival Horror, Mystery & Raccoon City

---

## What Is Resident Evil Requiem?

Resident Evil Requiem is a survival horror game from Capcom and the ninth main entry in the Resident Evil series. It combines tense exploration, environmental puzzles, resource management, investigation, and action in a dark narrative connected to the history of Raccoon City.

The game follows two central characters with distinct gameplay styles. Grace Ashcroft, an FBI intelligence analyst, investigates a mysterious death connected to an abandoned hotel. Leon S. Kennedy, a veteran agent familiar with the Raccoon City incident, investigates a series of deaths across the Midwest.

Their journeys intersect as the investigation uncovers connections to past events. The setting draws on the series' established history while introducing new mysteries, locations, and threats.

Built with Capcom's RE ENGINE, Resident Evil Requiem uses detailed environments, cinematic presentation, lighting, and character animation to create a tense atmosphere. Players can switch between first person and third person views, allowing them to experience encounters and exploration from different perspectives.

## Story and Setting

### A Mystery Connected to Raccoon City

The narrative begins with an investigation into mysterious deaths in the American Midwest. The trail leads toward an abandoned hotel and events connected to Raccoon City, a place central to the series' history.

The game balances new discoveries with familiar elements of the Resident Evil universe. Environmental clues, documents, locations, and character encounters help reveal how the current investigation relates to past disasters.

### Grace Ashcroft

Grace is an FBI intelligence analyst whose work relies on observation, reasoning, and careful investigation. Her chapters emphasize vulnerability, suspense, environmental awareness, and the challenge of navigating dangerous spaces with limited resources.

### Leon S. Kennedy

Leon returns as a seasoned agent whose sections place greater emphasis on direct encounters and action. His experience contrasts with Grace's investigative perspective, giving the two sides of the narrative distinct rhythms and gameplay demands.

## Gameplay Features

### Survival Horror

Players must balance exploration, puzzle-solving, encounters, and resource management. Careful decisions matter because supplies and safe opportunities can be limited, making preparation an important part of survival.

### Two Playable Characters

Grace and Leon bring different strengths and styles to the adventure. Their connected narratives alternate between suspenseful investigation and more action-focused sequences, creating variety across the campaign.

### First Person and Third Person Views

The game allows players to switch between first person and third person perspectives. First person emphasizes close observation and an immediate sense of danger, while third person offers a wider view of the character and surrounding environment.

### Investigation and Puzzles

Clues and environmental details help players understand locations and uncover information. Puzzles add a layer of reasoning between tense encounters, rewarding attention to the surroundings rather than constant movement.

### Resource Management

Inventory decisions and the careful use of available supplies are central to the survival horror structure. Players must assess risks, explore thoroughly, and decide when to advance or conserve resources.

### Cinematic Presentation

RE ENGINE supports detailed character animation, lighting, textures, and atmospheric environments. These visual elements reinforce the contrast between quiet investigation and sudden moments of danger.

## Raccoon City and Series Lore

Raccoon City remains a major part of the Resident Evil identity. Requiem revisits the consequences of the outbreak and connects its new investigation to the wider history of the series.

Returning players can recognize familiar themes and historical references, while newcomers can follow Grace and Leon as they investigate the current mystery. The narrative brings together personal stories, unresolved events, and the long-lasting impact of the Raccoon City incident.
